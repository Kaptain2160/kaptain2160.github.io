Debra Eldin and Mountain Home Realty provide the same professional service to every person. This page is a plain-language summary of that commitment. It is general information, not legal advice.

Fair Housing Act

The federal Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination in the sale or rental of housing, and in related services, because of:

  • Race
  • Color
  • National origin
  • Religion
  • Sex, which the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development treats as including sexual orientation and gender identity
  • Familial status, including families with children under 18 and people who are pregnant
  • Disability

Tennessee Human Rights Act

The Tennessee Human Rights Act prohibits discriminatory housing practices because of race, color, creed, religion, sex, disability, familial status, or national origin. That list is in Tenn. Code Ann. § 4-21-601. The Tennessee Attorney General’s Civil Rights Enforcement Division also describes the Act’s housing protections as covering age.

Equal Housing Opportunity

We are pledged to the letter and spirit of equal housing opportunity. We encourage and support a program in which there are no barriers to obtaining housing because of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, creed, or age.
