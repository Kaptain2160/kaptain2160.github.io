Privacy Policy

This page describes what this website actually does with information. It is general information about the site, not legal advice.

Information is not sold

Debra Eldin does not sell personal information collected through this website.

Contact forms

The homepage and the Contact page each have a form. Both send what you submit to Formspree at formspree.io/f/xpqeaarv. This site does not keep its own database of those messages. Formspree receives the submission and delivers it to Debra.

The homepage form asks for your name, phone, email, what you are interested in, and a message. If you choose “Home valuation,” it also asks for a property address. The Contact page form asks for your first name, last name, email, phone (optional), what you are interested in, and a message.

Each form includes a hidden field that visitors are not asked to fill in. It is there so automated spam can be filtered. On the Contact page that field is named _gotcha, which is the name Formspree uses to drop spam. On the homepage, the page checks its hidden field in the browser and does not send the form if that field has been filled in.

Showing requests

Some listing cards link to Calendly at calendly.com/debraeldinrealtor/30min so you can book a showing or ask about showings. The link opens Calendly in a new tab and includes the property address. Anything you enter there is handled by Calendly, not by this website.

Analytics

This site’s code does not include an analytics service, advertising pixel, or script that saves information in your browser. It does not set its own cookies.

The pages do request a few outside files. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. The About page photo and the certification logos are loaded from Squarespace’s image host. Your browser’s ordinary request for those files can include your IP address. Some property cards also link to an outside listing page, and the site links to Instagram and Facebook. Those are ordinary links. This site does not embed their content or run their tracking pixels.

Asking Debra to delete information

To ask Debra Eldin to delete information she received from you through this site, email debraeldinrealtor@gmail.com, call (865) 806-7541, or call the office at (865) 366-4600. She will delete the messages she has. If you also entered information on Calendly, that copy is stored by Calendly, and you can contact Calendly about it.
