About Debra

A realtor who truly knows the mountains

Homeownership is one of the most monumental accomplishments in a person's life, and I am eager to get started assisting you in your journey.

With deep roots in East Tennessee and a genuine passion for this breathtaking community, I bring warmth, local expertise, and unwavering dedication to every client relationship — whether you're buying your first home, relocating from out of state, or selling the home you've loved for decades.

I believe that real estate is ultimately about people, not property. My job is to listen, understand your goals, and walk alongside you every step of the way — from the first showing to the closing table and beyond.
