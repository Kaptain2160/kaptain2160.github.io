Meet Debra Eldin

With more than three decades of life in East Tennessee, Debra Eldin brings a local perspective to helping families, investors, and second-home buyers find their place in the Smoky Mountains. From Sevierville to Gatlinburg to Pigeon Forge, she brings deep local knowledge and a genuinely personal touch to every transaction.

Buying from a distance? Debra helps you explore homes through video tours and keeps you informed along the way. Selling a home or navigating a land purchase? She takes time to work through the details, answer your questions, and advocate for your interests.
