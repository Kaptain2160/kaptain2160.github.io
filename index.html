Real estate isn’t just
her career — it’s her calling

As the daughter of two seasoned real estate brokers, she was immersed in the industry from day one — making real estate not just a career, but a calling. With 12 years as a licensed Realtor and over 30 years of East Tennessee living, she brings a powerful combination of legacy, local expertise, and a deep-rooted commitment to service.

A full-service agent, she works with buyers, sellers, relocations, and investors — offering expertise in financing options including 1031 exchanges, DSCR, USDA, FHA, VA, and first-time homebuyer programs, as well as specialized markets like overnight rental cabins.

More than just a Realtor, she is a matchmaker, detective, and trusted advisor — pairing clients with the right home, uncovering every detail, and guiding them through each step with care and confidence. She believes the highest compliment she can receive is a referral — earned through trust, results, and an exceptional client experience.
