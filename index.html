Meet Debra Eldin

For over three decades, Debra Eldin has helped families, investors, and second-home buyers find their place in the Smoky Mountains. From Sevierville to Gatlinburg to Pigeon Forge, she brings deep local knowledge and a genuinely personal touch to every transaction.

Whether you're searching for a full-time residence, a short-term rental investment, or your family's mountain retreat, Debra's approach is the same: listen first, guide honestly, and never stop advocating for your best interest.
