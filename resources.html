Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to buy a home?

On average, the process from pre-approval to closing takes 45–60 days once you've found a home. Finding the right home can take anywhere from a few weeks to several months depending on your criteria and the market.

Do I need a realtor to buy a home?

Technically no, but having an experienced buyer's agent costs you nothing (the seller typically pays agent commissions) and gives you expert negotiation, local market knowledge, and someone in your corner throughout the process.

What areas does Debra serve?

Debra serves the greater East Tennessee area including Maryville, Sevierville, Gatlinburg, Alcoa, Knoxville, and surrounding communities throughout the Smoky Mountain region.

How do I get started?

The easiest first step is a free, no-pressure conversation with Debra. Call, text, or send a message through the contact page and she'll get back to you promptly.
